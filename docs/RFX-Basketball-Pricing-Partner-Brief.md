## 1. The decision on the table

From the Aug 11 meeting: move to a revenue model that charges **both sides of the market** — athletes/clubs *and* institutions — instead of athlete-only. This brief turns that decision into three concrete price points for basketball and shows the market data behind each one.

### Where RFX pricing stands today (for reference)

- **RFX Soccer, live on the App Store:** $150/mo · $299.99/yr · $299.99 lifetime · $199 "Exposure" · **$499 club Whitelabeling**
- **OPTIQ premium services (positioned, not yet at scale):** Studio reel from $299 · Advisor from $1,499 · Scout Match from $4,900/yr
- **OPTIQ deck's own coach-seat model:** $500–$5K per coach seat/yr sold to college programs — the proposed $2,500/program basketball price sits mid-band of our own model
- **Basketball is a net-new SKU** — the platform is soccer-only in code today, so these prices are a greenfield decision, not an adjustment

## 2. Proposed basketball pricing

| Tier | Who pays | Price | What they get |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B2C — Athlete/Family** | Individual athletes | **$34.99/mo or $349/yr** | Profile, AI outreach engine, engagement analytics |
| **B2B — Club/Program** | AAU & grassroots clubs | **$1,495/yr license + $99/athlete/yr** (min. 10 seats) | Club dashboard, roster sync/automation, seats at ~70% off retail |
| **College Coach/Program** | College staffs | **$2,500/yr per program** (dept. pricing custom) | Verified athlete feed, filters, direct contact, priority data delivery |

## 3. Why these numbers (comp-anchored)

- **$349/yr athlete** sits mid-band in the proven $290–$500/yr software sweet spot: SportsRecruits $399/yr, FieldLevel Gold $470/yr, ProductiveRecruit $290/yr, CaptainU Gold ~$480/yr. It is ~10x below NCSA's $2,000–$6,000 human-service contracts while automating the work those contracts pay humans to do.
- **$1,495 + $99/seat club** lands inside budgets clubs already carry — Hudl basketball club tiers are $400/$1,000/$1,600 per team/yr — and mirrors the wholesale-seat mechanics clubs know: CaptainU Teams sells 25 seats for $500 ($20/seat), SportsRecruits club seats pass through at ~$250/seat. Our $99 seat is ~70% off our own retail, the same discount structure FieldLevel Prime+ uses.
- **$2,500/yr college program** is the published floor of a quote-only category: Front Rush runs ~$3,000–$10,000+/yr per department; Teamworks (ARMS) sells five-figure D1 bundles. Nobody publishes a price — we will, and we aim it at the D2/D3/NAIA/high-academic segment we're targeting, which the enterprise suites overserve and overcharge.

## 4. The whitespace we're claiming

1. **Price gap:** No incumbent sells an AI-first product between the ~$30/mo software ceiling and the $2,000+ human-service floor. AI is either given away (highlight tools, ~$50/reel) or hidden inside human-service margins (NCSA).
2. **Transparency gap:** Every coach-side and club-side incumbent is quote-only. Published pricing is itself a sales weapon and a trust signal.
3. **Both-sides gap:** Incumbents monetize one side (families) and give the other side away. Charging coaches filters the network to programs actively recruiting — better matches, and revenue per college program that athlete-only models leave on the table.

## 5. Illustrative revenue math

- One mid-size AAU program (8 teams, ~96 athletes): $1,495 + 96 × $99 ≈ **$11,000/yr**
- 10 such clubs: ≈ **$110,000 ARR**
- 20 college programs at $2,500: **$50,000 ARR**
- 200 direct B2C athletes at $349: ≈ **$70,000 ARR**
- Referral loop (from meeting): youth-program partnerships with structured referral incentives feed the B2C and club funnels; club seats priced so directors can bundle into dues.

## 6. Cost-context talking points (for club conversations)

- Serious AAU families spend $400–$4,000+ per summer; shoe-circuit families $10,000+.
- One tournament weekend: $200–$600 entry.
- A $99–$349/yr recruiting seat is the smallest line in the basketball budget and the only one that directly generates college conversations.
